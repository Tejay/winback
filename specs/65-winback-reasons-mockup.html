Rules in plain English

  • ✅ An improvement is a real product improvement — confirmed at add-time, confirmed at remove-time.
  • ✅ Each improvement lives for 12 months, then auto-rolls off. Older ones go quietly without merchant action.
  • ✅ Up to 10 active improvements at any time.
  • ✅ Every improvement is linked to a customer demand signal (the roadmap panel). Pre-emptive improvements without a signal require explicit acknowledgement.
  • ✅ Removing requires two checked boxes — confirming the feature is no longer real AND acknowledging that customers already told can't be un-told.
  • ✅ Merchants nudged at most every 60 days.
  • ✅ The roadmap signal sits above the editor — improving what customers actually want, not guessing.
  • ✅ Reach shown before publish — sidebar shows AI-estimated count of customers who'll hear about it over the 12-month life. No individual names.
  • ✅ Each customer hears about an improvement at most once. The system handles the spacing; the merchant doesn't have to think about it.
  • ✅ Customers see real improvements, not marketing. AI quality gate at write time blocks vague entries (e.g. "we improved performance").
